# killallgit

We help ambitious professionals become AI Prompt Engineers™ by using AI to write the prompts that prove they are AI Prompt Engineers™.

Why spend years developing judgment when a large language model can produce a 900-word prompt explaining exactly how much judgment you have?

## Our methodology

- Tell our AI you want to be seen as an AI Prompt Engineer™.
- Receive a bespoke prompt about strategic prompting, systems thinking, and “unlocking leverage.”
- Paste it into another AI, then publish the result as your framework.

## What you get

- A prompt that is longer than the task.
- A prompt persona with an impressive but unverifiable job title.
- A tasteful amount of ALL CAPS for the LinkedIn announcement.
- Complete freedom from having to ask the AI a normal question in normal language.

## The future is prompt-shaped

The best prompt engineers do not write prompts. They delegate prompt writing to AI, then provide high-value human oversight by choosing whether the rocket emoji goes before or after “game changer.”
