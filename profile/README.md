# killallgit

We help teams keep AI slop out of the work that matters by applying a carefully managed amount of AI slop to it first.

Our systems read the drafts, summaries, follow-ups, roadmaps, and thought leadership so your team can spend less time asking whether a paragraph was generated by a person, a model, or a person using a model while pretending it was neither.

## How it works

- Feed the slop into our AI.
- Let our AI identify the slop with confidence calibrated by more AI.
- Receive a clean, concise explanation of why the original should probably not have been sent.

## Our promise

We believe the future of work should contain fewer hollow sentences, fewer urgent recaps, and at least one responsible adult between the prompt and the publish button.

## Work with us

If your team has an AI slop problem, we have an AI solution. This is either the breakthrough you were looking for or the final form of the problem. We are still iterating.
